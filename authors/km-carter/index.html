K.M. Carter

Her book with Bipedal Press will be announced soon.

K.M. Carter quit Advertising Age magazine in Chicago, backpacked across the country, let the Great Plains broaden her horizons, and wrote Love Nation (2018), a screenplay about the experience that garnered forty-six contest placements, including two first-place wins (Canadian Diversity Film Festival, West Field Screenwriting Awards). And while her forthcoming book with Bipedal Press also focuses on the South Dakota drowning murders of mostly Lakota men, it ponders a shared history and what reparation really means. Equally, it’s a product of deep research: Michigan State University’s creative writing program (three years), including resident advisor junior year; English and ancient Latin at the University of Illinois, Chicago (one year); and a post-Ad Age résumé that ventures from Maine’s Portland Press Herald to a fishing trawler in Dutch Harbor, Alaska. Carter lives in Georgia.
