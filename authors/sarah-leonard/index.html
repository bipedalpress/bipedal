Sarah Leonard

Her book with Bipedal Press will be announced soon.

Sarah Leonard’s roots are in Kentucky, where she was born and raised, and she has spent years living in Virginia and Tennessee, where some of the happiest of them were just outside Nashville. She now lives in California with her daughter, her partner Walter (whom she’s known since she was eleven), and an old dog named Stinky.

A lifelong reader and an unapologetic horror-novel devotee, Leonard writes dark, character-driven fiction steeped in Southern secrets: stories about the families who raise us, the darkness we inherit, and the people we root for even when we shouldn’t. When she isn’t writing, she’s spending time with her daughter, tending her ever-growing collection of vine plants, or hunting down her next favorite read.
