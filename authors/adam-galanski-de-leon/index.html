Adam Galanski-De León

His next book with Bipedal Press will be announced soon.

Adam Galanski-De León is the author of multiple books, including The Laughter of Hyenas at Bay and Bastards in Oxblood. He is the winner of the Buffalo Books Prize in Fiction 2022 and has been nominated various times for the Pushcart Prize. Adam lives in Chicago, IL with his wife and children.
