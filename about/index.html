Who we are

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher in Hickory, North Carolina, for books that make you look twice.

We publish work that challenges assumptions, unsettles the familiar, and invites readers to see the world differently. We’re here for readers who like their stories a little sideways, and for authors who break the mold.

How Bipedal Press began

Bipedal Press is the brainchild of Courtney (me) and the Other.

The Other is a manifestation of everything that lies unseen, unnoticed, unappreciated, and unrepresented in the world.

The Other is that thought you pushed away because it didn’t match convention.

The Other realized the word “bipedal” is a funny word used to describe humans, along with other creatures that are (for some reason) othered. How odd does it feel to describe yourself as bipedal? And even then—are you?

The Other saw the status quo, poked it, and asked, “wtf is this?”

I’m pretty sure that’s what they said when they saw me.

But then I started to steer away from the paths laid before me.

To skin convention, turn it inside out, and wear it like a mask.

To deflect the naysayers and embrace the wild, the wonderful, the wicked, and the weird.

The glyphs in our logo spell “bipedal.”
