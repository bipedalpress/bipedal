How to submit

What to send

Email a complete manuscript to courtney@bipedalpress.com along with:

  • A brief query introducing yourself and your work
  • A short synopsis of the book
  • The manuscript
  • Your contact information and approximate word count

Good to know

We accept simultaneous submissions, but please let us know if your manuscript is accepted elsewhere.

We do not accept previously published work unless the rights have reverted to the author.

If you’re not sure whether your work is a fit, that’s okay. We’d rather you ask than count yourself out.

Questions, answered.

What makes Bipedal different?

We illuminate. We work with you collaboratively, side by side, to pull your book out of the void and bring it into focus.

Do you charge authors fees?

No. Bipedal Press never charges authors fees.

Do you pay royalties?

Yes. We pay fair royalties, because we celebrate human creativity and ingenuity.

How fast will I hear back?

We reply to every email within two days.

What kinds of books are you looking for?

Literary fiction, speculative fiction, fantasy, horror, dark academia, or something that refuses to pick a box. We have a soft spot for dark academia, but genre matters less to us than voice. If your book shows us the world in a way we never considered, send it.

What should I send?

A complete manuscript, emailed to courtney@bipedalpress.com, with a short note about you and your book, a brief synopsis, and your word count.

Do you read works in progress?

We read complete manuscripts only.

Can I submit to other publishers at the same time?

Yes. Simultaneous submissions are fine. Just tell us if someone else says yes first.

Do you accept previously published work?

Not unless the rights have reverted to you.

Do you accept work that was written with AI?

We welcome authors who use technology as part of their creative process, but the submitted work must ultimately be your own. We do not accept manuscripts that are substantially generated by AI.

What does editing look like?

Mitts-on. We dig into your book to help make it the best version of itself. We use author-friendly processes that prioritize your vision, and we keep you informed at every stage.

Will I have a say in my cover?

Yes, if you want one. We design covers in collaboration with authors.

Who handles layout and production?

We do, in-house. Interior layout, cover design, and sound editing for audiobooks all happen here.

Are you publishing books yet?

Our first titles are on the way. Join the newsletter to hear about them first.

I’m not sure my book fits. Should I still ask?

Please do. We’d rather hear from you than have you count yourself out. We’re all about filling in the margins, after all.

A note about AI

Bipedal Press publishes work created by upright apes. Humans.

We welcome authors who use technology as part of their creative process, but the submitted work must ultimately be the author’s own. We do not accept manuscripts that are substantially generated by AI.

We believe the human voice—the strange, messy, surprising thing that’s entirely yours—is worth protecting.
