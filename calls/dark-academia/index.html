Flying Too Close to the Sun

Now accepting dark academia manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher in Hickory, North Carolina, and dark academia is the genre we love most, so we’re accepting dark academia manuscripts right now and we’d be thrilled to read yours.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

We love dark academia for its Icarus-like lessons, for the way brilliant people climb a little too high on wings they built themselves, and we’re drawn to novels about the scholar who stays in the archive long after closing, the friend group guarding a secret, or the professor everyone admires and nobody thinks to question, and we’d love to see the secret society, the forbidden collection, the rivalry that goes a step too far, and the beautiful old building that’s falling apart around all of them.

Dark academia, dark academia adjacent, and dark academia with fantasy elements are all welcome here. Your story doesn’t need a campus at all, since a research station, a conservatory, a monastery library, or a very strange book club will do just as well, as long as it has the devotion, the beauty, and the rot.

What matters most to us is character, because we want to sit inside the head of someone brilliant who is about to make a very bad decision and understand exactly why it made sense to them at the time. The mood is the easy part, and what really interests us is what’s going on underneath it, whether that’s class, power, ambition, who gets to teach and who gets taught, or the stories institutions tell about themselves.

What we’re hoping to find

  • Character-driven, psychological work with a narrator we can’t quite stop listening to.
  • An obsession with a subject, a person, or an idea.
  • A setting that matters to the plot as much as it matters to the mood.
  • Secrets, rivalries, and the consequences that follow them.
  • Mythology, folklore, the occult, or a little magic, if it belongs in your world.

You might belong here if

you have a favorite dead language, your narrator is the smartest person in the room and slightly wrong about that, or there’s a basement in your book that nobody should go into.

Not for us

a pretty campus with nothing sketchy, mood standing in for character, or a manuscript that hasn’t been edited and proofread yet.

Questions

Does my book need to be set at a university?

Not at all, because if it has the devotion, the beauty, and the rot, we’d love to read it, wherever it happens to take place.

Do you take dark academia with fantasy elements?

Yes, along with dark academia adjacent, and we’re especially fond of the ones that are character-driven and psychological.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Dark Academia.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
