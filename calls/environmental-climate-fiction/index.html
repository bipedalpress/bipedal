The Riverbed Is Barren

Now accepting environmental and climate fiction manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher, and we’re accepting environmental and climate fiction, so if your novel is about the living world, or about people trying to live in a world that’s changing around them, we’d love to read it.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

That covers a lot of ground, from a flood or a drought to a garden, a river, a species disappearing, a farm that can’t go on, a family that has to move, or a city adapting as best it can. It could be set tomorrow or a century from now, and it could be literary, speculative, horror, or something that doesn’t have a name yet.

We want it told through people, so the grief should be real, and so should the humor, the family dinners, and the arguments about nothing. Your characters don’t need to be heroes or experts, only believable and wanting something, and while hope is welcome we don’t require it.

We’re also curious about who gets heard in these stories, including farmers, workers, kids, elders, and people who live closest to the land, alongside the scientists and activists.

What we’re hoping to find

  • Stories about land, water, animals, and weather.
  • Ordinary people in extraordinary conditions.
  • Quiet, strange, and speculative approaches.
  • Grief and humor in the same scene.
  • Voices from the communities that live closest to the change.

You might belong here if

your book is about a flood, a garden, a move, or a family learning to live with less.

Not for us

lectures, one scientist who saves everyone, or disaster as pure spectacle.

Questions

What counts as environmental fiction?

Any story where the living world matters to the plot, from a single backyard to the whole planet.

Does it have to be hopeful?

No.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Environmental and Climate Fiction.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
