The House Has Been in the Family Too Long

Now accepting Southern and Appalachian gothic manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher in Hickory, North Carolina, so this call is close to home, and we’re accepting Southern and Appalachian gothic novels that we’d love to read.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

We’re hoping for family secrets, inherited guilt, and strange things behind old doors, all wrapped in local color that feels earned, whether that’s the heat, the church basement, the creek nobody swims in anymore, or the house that’s been in the family far too long. The place should matter as much as the people in it.

The supernatural can be real, imagined, or left deliberately open, and we care about the people more than the ghost. We like characters who are complicated, and we like stories that look honestly at the South’s history and its present, told by many kinds of Southern voices.

What we’re hoping to find

  • Place as a character.
  • Family secrets and inherited guilt.
  • Haunted houses, haunted towns, and haunted people.
  • Local color that feels earned.
  • Humor that sneaks up on you.

You might belong here if

your grandmother’s house is in the book, your church knows too much, or somebody in the family history is better left undiscussed.

Not for us

a postcard South with nothing underneath, or stereotypes with no point of view behind them.

Questions

Do I have to be from the South?

No, though the place has to feel lived in.

Is Appalachian horror welcome?

Yes, along with Southern gothic and folk horror.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Southern Gothic.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
