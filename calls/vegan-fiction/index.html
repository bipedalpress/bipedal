The Chicken Has a Story

Now accepting vegan fiction manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher, and we’re accepting vegan fiction, so if you’ve written a novel that takes animals, and our relationship with food, seriously, we’d love to read it.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

Most fiction is about people, and most of the animals in it are there as decoration: dogs die so we’ll cry, chickens only exist in the background of a farm scene, and wolves are guides, so we’d like to read the book where the animal is a life of its own.

It might be a dystopia built on industrial farming, or a story narrated by a cow, a crow, or a colony of bees, or a family dinner that goes sideways in ways nobody planned for, and it doesn’t have to be about veganism or feature a vegan protagonist, although it does have to take seriously the lives of beings who aren’t us.

Literary, speculative, fantasy, horror, and mystery are all welcome, and humor is too, and if your book makes a reader look at their dinner or their dog a little differently, we’re very interested.

What we’re hoping to find

  • Animals as full characters rather than symbols.
  • Food systems, farms, sanctuaries, and kitchens as settings with real consequences.
  • Animal narrators and multispecies worlds.
  • Characters who change their minds, or who refuse to.

You might belong here if

you’ve written the scene at the slaughterhouse, the sanctuary, or the kitchen table that changed everything.

Not for us

sermons, or a vegan character who exists only to be right.

Questions

Does my protagonist have to be vegan?

No, and the book doesn’t have to be about veganism either.

Do you take speculative and horror?

Yes, both.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Vegan Fiction.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
