Open calls

Told by Something That Isn’t Human

Now accepting speculative fiction manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher, and we’re accepting speculative fiction, with a special weakness for stories told from outside the human point of view.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

Maybe your narrator is a machine, an animal, a ghost, a colony, or a god who’s a little bit bored, and we’d love to see the world the way that narrator sees it, including the parts of human life that make no sense to them at all. The strangeness should live in the way of seeing and not only in the setting.

Voice matters more to us than rules, so we want a character who’s alive from the very first page, and if page one is all worldbuilding it probably isn’t for us, since we’d much rather be handed a narrator and trust you with the map later.

Dystopian worlds are welcome here too, and so are stories that wander between science fiction, fantasy, and horror, as long as they engage with the world and not just the technology.

What we’re hoping to find

  • A non-human narrator with a real voice.
  • A strange, lived-in logic.
  • Humor, grief, and tenderness from unexpected places.
  • Stories that say something true about how people treat the world.

You might belong here if

your narrator doesn’t understand why humans do what they do, or you needed a new species to say something true about people.

Not for us

a human story with a robot sidekick, or invented jargon standing in for character.

Questions

Does my narrator have to be non-human?

No, though it’s one of our favorite approaches, and dystopian worlds are welcome too.

Do you take science fiction and fantasy?

Yes, when it’s character-driven.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Speculative Fiction.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
