Somebody Was Left Out of the Story

Now accepting mythological retelling manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher, and we’re accepting mythological retellings, so if you’ve taken an old story and asked it a different question, we’d love to read it.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

We all know how these stories go, or at least we think we do, and what we want is the version told by someone who was left out of the first telling, like the monster, the servant, the sister, the wife, or the one who was only mentioned in passing. We’re especially interested in characters who have been overlooked or underrepresented, and in what changes when they finally get to speak.

Myths and legends from anywhere in the world are welcome, along with folklore and fairy tales, and you don’t have to stay faithful to the original, although you do have to give us a reason to look again. A good retelling poses a new question, and the answer should surprise us a little.

We like retellings that are character-driven and a bit uncomfortable, and that ask who got to tell the story the first time around.

What we’re hoping to find

  • A familiar story seen through someone we don’t usually hear from.
  • A new question the original never asked.
  • Myths and legends from beyond the usual places.
  • A strong, distinct voice.

You might belong here if

you’ve always suspected the villain was misunderstood, or you love a myth too much to simply repeat it.

Not for us

a faithful retelling with new names, or a retelling that leaves the original’s power structure unquestioned.

Questions

Which myths do you want?

Any, from anywhere, and we’d love to be surprised.

Do you take novels, or only short stories?

We read complete novel manuscripts.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Mythological Retelling.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
