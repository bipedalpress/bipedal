A Dystopia Too Close for Comfort

Now accepting dystopian fiction manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher, and we’re accepting dystopian fiction, so if you’ve built a world that runs on rules that are cruel and also, somehow, logical, we’d love to read it.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

The best dystopias are the ones where you can see how we got there, and we’re hungry for the small details, like what people eat, what they whisper, what they’ve stopped noticing, which jobs still exist, and which words are no longer safe to say out loud. A reader should be able to picture an ordinary Tuesday in your world and feel slightly sick about it.

We care more about the person than the system, which means we want someone with something to lose, and a world that presses on them a little more each day. Your protagonist doesn’t have to be the chosen one, and they can just as easily be someone trying to get to work who keeps noticing things they wish they hadn’t. We like dystopias that question the way things are, and we’re interested in who benefits, who pays, and who looks away.

What we’re hoping to find

  • A society with its own believable logic.
  • A character with something to lose.
  • Everyday life under extraordinary control.
  • Science-fiction and speculative approaches, as long as they have something to say.
  • Endings that don’t tie everything up too neatly.

You might belong here if

your protagonist isn’t the chosen one, you wrote it before you saw the headline, or your world has a rule that’s absurd and also kind of sensible.

Not for us

a world used only as backdrop, a rebellion plot with no people in it, or science fiction with nothing to say.

Questions

Does my book need a rebellion?

No, because simply existing in a dystopia is enough to make us think, so your characters don’t need to rise up against anything for the book to work.

Do you take science fiction?

Yes, as long as it has something to say about the world.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Dystopian Fiction.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
