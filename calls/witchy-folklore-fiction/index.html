Open calls

Nevertheless, she persisted.

Now accepting witchy, folklore, and wild woman fiction

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher, and we’re accepting manuscripts about women who refuse to behave, so if you’ve written a witch, a healer, a monster, or a woman who has turned into something strange along the way, we’d love to read it.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

Somewhere in your book there’s a woman who was supposed to stay quiet and didn’t, and maybe she’s a witch, or a mother who finally ran out of patience, or a healer the village needs and fears, or one of those monsters who act with very good reason, and we’d love to spend a few hundred pages with her.

Folklore, fantasy, horror, literary fiction, and magical realism are all welcome, along with anything that doesn’t fit neatly into a single category, and we’re drawn to power, appetite, aging, rage, friendship between women, and the strange business of being told what a woman should be. She doesn’t have to be likable, although she does have to be entirely her own.

We care a great deal about how she’s written, which means her power shouldn’t be punished by the plot and the book shouldn’t be built to admire her body more than her mind. We don’t publish work that condones sexism or objectifies women, and to be clear, characters can behave badly and many stories need them to, but the book itself can’t carry that attitude.

You don’t need a literal witch for this call, though a little witchcraft in the bones is always welcome.

What we’re hoping to find

  • Women with real power and real appetite.
  • Folklore, herbalism, folk magic, and old stories used with care.
  • Rage, grief, aging, motherhood, and friendship, all in the same book if it needs them.
  • Atmosphere and character over spectacle.

You might belong here if

your heroine has been called “too much,” she knows things she can’t quite explain, or she would honestly rather be feared than rescued.

Not for us

witches as costume, or work that condones sexism or objectifies women.

Questions

Does my book need an actual witch?

No, a little witchcraft in the bones is plenty.

Do you take retellings and fairy tales?

Yes, and you can see our mythological retellings call for more on that.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Witchy and Folklore Fiction.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
