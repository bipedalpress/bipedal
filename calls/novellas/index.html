Got a Novella? We’d Love to Read It

Now accepting novella manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher, and we’re accepting novellas, because some stories are exactly as long as they need to be and we’d love to read yours.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

We’re looking for novellas of roughly 20,000 to 50,000 words with one strong idea and a voice you’ll still hear after you close the book. A novella can do things a novel can’t, like staying in one room, one night, or one terrible season, and we like them tight, strange, and finished.

We’ll edit a novella as carefully as we would a novel, and we’ll handle the cover, the layout, and the audiobook in-house, since short books tend to make wonderful audiobooks.

What we’re hoping to find

  • One strong idea, told well.
  • A voice with real character.
  • Compression and control.
  • Any genre we publish.

You might belong here if

your story is too big for a short story and too lean for a novel.

Not for us

a novel cut in half, or a short story stretched out.

Questions

How long is a novella?

For us, roughly 20,000 to 50,000 words.

Do you take story collections?

Not at this time, though you can subscribe to our newsletter to hear if that changes.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Novella.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
