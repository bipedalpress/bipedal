There’s Something in the Woods

Now accepting folk horror and eco-horror manuscripts

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher, and we’re accepting folk horror and eco-horror, so if your novel has a village, a forest, or a field with a bad reputation, we’d love to read it.

Email your manuscriptSubmission guidelines

We’re looking for stories where the place itself is the danger, whether that’s the marsh, the mountain, the farm, or the town with the odd harvest festival, along with old customs, strange rituals, and communities that are a little too welcoming. The dread should come from the land and from the people who live on it.

Folk horror asks what a community will do to keep itself going, and eco-horror asks what the natural world might do back, and we love both, especially when they come with a character we care about who walks in a little too curious and stays a little too long.

What we’re hoping to find

  • Rituals and communities with a logic of their own.
  • Land and weather as active forces.
  • Outsiders, insiders, and the people who can’t leave.
  • Dread that comes from the place itself.

You might belong here if

your book has a festival, a fence, or a tree nobody’s allowed to touch.

Not for us

a nature lecture with a monster attached, or a cult with no real people inside it.

Questions

What’s the difference between folk horror and eco-horror?

Folk horror leans on community and tradition, while eco-horror leans on the natural world, and we read both.

Can it be set anywhere?

Yes, as long as the place feels real.

Why Bipedal Press

We’re an independent press in Hickory, North Carolina, and you’ll work with us directly on the edit, the cover, and the layout, with the audiobook handled in-house as well.

  • No fees, ever, and we pay fair royalties.
  • Hands-on editing, where we dig into your book and keep you informed at every stage.
  • Cover, layout, and audiobook all done in-house, so your book has one consistent vision from the first page to the final recording.
  • A real person on the other end, because if we’re interested we’ll set up a video chat to talk about your book and what comes next.

Send us your manuscript

Email submit@bipedalpress.com with the subject line “Manuscript submission: Folk Horror and Eco-Horror.” You don’t need an agent. We’ll acknowledge your submission within two days, and although decisions may take longer than that, we’ll let you know if we’re still reading. We start with the beginning, and if it intrigues us we’ll read the end as well. We read complete manuscripts only, and simultaneous submissions are fine, as long as you let us know if someone else says yes first. See our submission guidelines for what to include.
