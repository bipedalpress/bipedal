Book description

No one expects the bomb to drop.

A firing-squad execution of seventeen soldiers puts rideshare driver Victor Hartwell’s hometown of Foster on the national stage, and makes traffic hell.

Pushing through chronic sleep deprivation and dreaming of a call he knows won’t come, Vic hustles to pay back an impossible debt, or at least catch a movie. His back seat is a cross-section of a city consumed by whispers of civil war, the seductive solace of synthetic relationships, and the tightening threads of megacorps as people escape into Ether.

Here, strangers connect and unravel. Through his rearview mirror, Vic becomes entangled in the undoing of some, the stubborn persistence of others, and the insulated glamour of executives thriving in the chaos.

RIDESHARE weaves Vic’s story with found footage, in-world documents, and testimonies that reveal the widening cracks of society on a day that everyone will remember—when all Vic wants to do is forget.

About Bipedal Press

Bipedal Press is an independent publisher in Hickory, North Carolina, with one aim: to illuminate good ideas and brilliant authors. We work with writers collaboratively, from first edit to final cover, to pull the strange out of the void and bring it into focus. RIDESHARE by David Ridd is our first book.
