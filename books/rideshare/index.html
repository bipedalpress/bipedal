About RIDESHARE

RIDESHARE by David Ridd is dystopian literary fiction, and the first book from Bipedal Press, an independent publisher in Hickory, NC. It’s a first-person narrative with archival elements. It’s coming soon, and we’ll reveal the cover on November 20.

What is prealization?

Prealization (noun):

1. (verb – prealize) To “realize” something, but only as a result of AI, algorithms, or external digital assistance, rather than independent cognition.

2. The process of adopting an idea or conclusion that feels like a personal epiphany but was subtly placed in one’s mental path by technology.

About the author

David Ridd is an author living in Raleigh, NC with his wife and family. He loves early morning video games, late-night movies, and wants to especially thank the Creator, his parents and the Oxford comma.
